## Brute (Mantellian Savrip / Kintan Strider)

MOVE: 1 tile forward, backward, left, right, or diagonally. When moving backward on the outer circle this piece can move as if it was going off the board to the other side.
ATTACK: 1 tile forward, left, right, or diagonally. Cannot attack pieces that have not moved from their starting position.

## Predator (K’lor’slug / Ng’ok)

MOVE: 2 tiles to the left or 2 tiles to the right. This piece can hop over other pieces. 1 tile forward or backward.
ATTACK: 1 tile forward or backward then 2 tiles to the right or the left (an L-shaped move).

## Guardian (Molator / Monnok)

MOVE: 1 tile forward or backward 2 tiles to the right or the left (an L-shaped move).
ATTACK: 2 tiles to the right or the left.

## Scout (Ghhhk / Houjix)

MOVE: 2 tiles in any direction. This piece can move off the start point and back again.
ATTACK: 1 square forward or backward. Can attack before or after moving, but can only attack once.
