# Instructions for Dejarik

Dejarik is a two-player game in which teams of creatures battle each other on a circular board. Dejarik is played on starships and in homes throughout the galaxy. The holographic version of the game is commonly known as Holochess.

## Object:
The object of the game is to capture all of your opponent’s pieces.

## Includes:
- Dejarik game board  
- 8 creature pieces  

## Board Layout:
The game is played on a circular board divided into three concentric circles. The outer and middle circles are each divided into twelve checkered tiles, while the inner circle is divided into four tiles.  
The game features 8 distinct pieces. The pieces are divided into two teams – the pieces on each team are visually similar and have the same moves and attacks. One player takes control of the Blue Team and the other player controls the Red Team. The players can move their pieces to attack one another.

## Gameplay:
- The game is played by two people.  
- The youngest player goes first.  
- Each player has a team of 4 pieces. The Blue Team has a blue base and the Red Team has a red base.  
- Your goal is to capture all of your opponent’s pieces.  
- Players take turns moving or attacking using one piece on their team.  
- Each piece on the team has a unique way of moving and a separate way of attacking. During each turn a player can choose to move or attack.  
- If your opponent’s piece is in an attack zone, then you can take this piece, moving your piece to the same location in the process.  
- When your piece attacks your opponent’s piece, your piece will move to occupy that location. Once in that location if another opponent’s piece is on a subsequent attack tile then you can take that piece too, chaining the attacks together.  
- The central circle cannot be occupied or moved across.  
- You must always move a piece when it is your turn. However, the Ghhhk and Houjix characters can move off the start point and back again because they have the ability to move 2 tiles in any direction.  
- Only 1 piece can occupy a tile at a time.  
- The characters on each team are evenly matched, and their moves and attacks are described in the chart.

## How the game ends:
The first player to lose all of his or her pieces loses the game.  
If you only have one piece remaining, and you fail to take your opponent’s piece in 3 turns, you lose the game. Each time you acquire an opponent’s piece the 3 turn counter is reset.
