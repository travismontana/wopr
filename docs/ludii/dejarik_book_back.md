# Instructions for Checkers

## Object:
The object of the game is to capture all of your opponent’s checkers.

## Includes:
- Checkers game board  
- 12 black Imperial checkers  
- 12 red Rebellion checkers  

## Board Layout:
Checkers is played on a 64 square board. Only the dark colored squares are used in gameplay. Each player has a team of 4 pieces. The Blue Team has a blue base on the pieces and the Red Team has a red base on the piece.

## Gameplay:
The player using the black Imperial checkers goes first.  
All starting checkers can move one space diagonally forward. You cannot move a checker backwards until it becomes a King, as described below.  

If one of your opponent’s checkers is on a forward diagonal next to one of your checkers, and the next space beyond the opponent’s checker is empty, then your checker must jump the opponent’s checker and land in the space beyond. Your opponent’s checker is captured and removed from the board.  

After making one jump, your checker might have another jump available from its new position. Your checker must take that jump too. It must continue to jump until there are no more jumps available. Regular checkers and checkers that have been kinged are allowed to make multiple jumps.  

If, at the start of a turn, more than one of your checkers has a jump available, then you may decide which one you will move. But once you have chosen one, it must take all the jumps that it can.  

When one of your checkers reaches the opposite side of the board, it becomes a King. A King cannot move out of the row until the next turn. A King can move forward and backward along the diagonals.

## How the game ends:
The first player to lose all of his or her checkers loses the game. If a player is put in a position where they cannot move, they lose. If neither player can move, and both players have the same number of pieces, the player with the most Kings wins. If the players have an equal number of pieces and the same number of Kings the game is a draw.
